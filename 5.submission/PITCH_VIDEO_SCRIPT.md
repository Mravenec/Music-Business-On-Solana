# Presentation video — script (2:00–3:00)

Colosseum: "A two-to-three-minute presentation video. This is one of the first resources judges review, so it should be clear, concise, and high quality." It is a **startup pitch**, not a demo: founder on camera, a few slides, 10–15 seconds of product. Judges score founder–market fit, insight, product and execution, market size, communication, viability and traction.

Lines marked **FOUNDER:** are yours to say in your own words — only you can speak to your background, your market and your traction. Never claim users or revenue you do not have; "we are live on DevNet and onboarding our first students" is a strong, honest line.
